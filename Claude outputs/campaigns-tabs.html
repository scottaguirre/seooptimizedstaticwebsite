Calls I made that you should check

  • “In progress” opens first, not “New campaign” — you check on things far more often than you start them. New campaign is last for the same reason.
  • Drafts got their own tab, called “Waiting for you” — your three tabs had nowhere to put a planned-but-unapproved campaign. It isn’t in progress, and it certainly isn’t completed. The amber count is the only one that means “do something”.
  • “Coming up” lives inside In progress — it only ever describes running campaigns, so it has no business on the other tabs.
  • The form gained the fields it was missing — how many posts, how often, what time — with the price and the finish date shown as you set them. Right now you cannot see what a campaign costs until after you plan it.
  • Completed shows what you got — which posts, when, and how many links each has. Plus “Plan another for this page”, which is what you actually want from a finished campaign.

Still just a preview. Tabs, campaign rows and the search-term toggle work; nothing else does. In WordPress these would be real tabs — ?page=interlink-engine&tab=drafts — so each is a bookmarkable page and a reload keeps you where you were.
