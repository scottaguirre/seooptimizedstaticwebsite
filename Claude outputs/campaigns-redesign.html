What changed

  • “Coming up” is gone — with one campaign it repeated the card underneath. It returns below, when it has something to say.
  • The new-campaign form is behind a button — you visit this page to check on things far more often than to start something.
  • One word per state — Scheduled, Arriving, Live. “Waiting to collect” and “not written yet” described the same post two ways.
  • “Publish now” became “Publish early” — a quiet link, because it discards the date you paid to plan.
  • Search terms moved behind a toggle — they matter when you are diagnosing, not when you are glancing.
